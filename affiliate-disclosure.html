How we make money

Straight answer: some of our product links pay us a small commission.

How it works

Lawn Obsessed USA is free. When the app or this site recommends a product, the "Buy" link may be an affiliate link. If you buy through it, the retailer pays us a small commission. It costs you nothing extra.

We work with retailers and brands including Amazon, DoMyOwn, The Andersons, Golf Course Lawn Store, Yard Mastery, Jonathan Green, Solutions Pest & Lawn, Simple Lawn Solutions and Soil Savvy. As an Amazon Associate we earn from qualifying purchases.

How we pick products

  • Products are picked on ingredients, quality and value for your lawn and your effort level.
  • No brand pays to be featured.

Every product in the app has a "Why we picked it" so you can judge the reasoning yourself.
